Our Story

Built on a Foundation of Trust

Founded in Dubai, ASAS ALENJAZ Technical Works LLC is a UAE-based technical services company specializing in comprehensive building maintenance, interior fit-out, and MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing) solutions.

The company is committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective services to residential, commercial, and industrial clients across the United Arab Emirates.

Over the years, we have grown into a full-service technical works company serving hundreds of clients across all seven emirates, with an unwavering commitment to quality and client satisfaction.

What We Offer

Complete Technical Solutions

Building Materials Supply

Construction & Infrastructure

We supply a comprehensive range of premium building materials from certified manufacturers, ensuring every product meets UAE standards and project specifications.

  • Structural steel, reinforcement bars & metal profiles
  • Portland cement, ready-mix concrete additives
  • Natural and engineered stone, tiles & flooring
  • Interior and exterior paints and coatings
  • Insulation materials — thermal, acoustic, fire-rated
  • Plumbing materials, pipes, fittings & fixtures

General Maintenance

Facilities Management

Comprehensive planned and reactive maintenance services for all property types, carried out by our team of certified technicians and engineers.

  • Annual maintenance contracts (AMC) for all property types
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling and reporting
  • Emergency repair services — 24/7 response
  • Facade and external structure maintenance
  • Common area and amenity upkeep
  • Asset management and lifecycle planning

MEP Services

Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing

End-to-end MEP solutions for new construction and refurbishment projects, delivered by licensed engineers to the highest technical standards.

  • Electrical installation, testing & commissioning
  • HVAC system installation and maintenance
  • Plumbing, drainage and sanitary works
  • Fire fighting and suppression systems
  • BMS and smart building system integration
  • Energy audit and optimization services

Painting & Finishing

Interior & Exterior

Professional painting and decorative finishing services using premium materials, with meticulous attention to surface preparation and final presentation.

  • Interior and exterior painting for all building types
  • Anti-corrosion and protective coatings
  • Decorative finishes — texture, stucco, Venetian plaster
  • Epoxy flooring and industrial coatings
  • Wallpaper installation and wall treatments

Waterproofing Solutions

Leak Prevention & Treatment

Advanced waterproofing systems to protect your structure from water ingress, using globally proven technologies backed by long-term warranties.

  • Roof waterproofing — flat, pitched & green roofs
  • Basement and underground waterproofing
  • Swimming pool and water tank lining
  • Terrace and podium waterproofing
  • Crack injection and structural waterproofing

Civil & Structural Works

Construction & Renovation

Structural and civil engineering works for new construction, extension, refurbishment, and demolition projects handled by our experienced site teams.

  • Concrete structures, columns and slabs
  • Partitioning — drywall, glass and aluminum
  • Flooring — tiles, marble, hardwood & epoxy
  • Fit-out and interior renovation works
  • Demolition and structural modifications

Home FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services, materials, and processes.

Help Center

Got Questions?

Building Materials
What types of building materials do you supply?
We supply a comprehensive range including structural steel, cement, aggregates, tiles, natural stone, paints, insulation, plumbing materials, electrical cables, and specialty construction products. We work with leading local and international manufacturers to ensure premium quality.
What is your minimum order quantity?
We cater to projects of all sizes — from small villa renovations to major infrastructure developments. There is no fixed minimum order; contact our sales team for a customized quote based on your requirements.
Do you offer material samples before ordering?
Yes, we provide material samples for most product categories including tiles, paints, stone, and insulation products. Simply contact our sales team or visit our showroom to view samples and specifications before placing your order.
Are your materials compliant with UAE building standards?
Absolutely. All materials we supply are compliant with UAE Ministry of Infrastructure Development standards, Dubai Municipality requirements, and relevant international standards (ASTM, BS, EN). We provide full material certificates and test reports on request.
Delivery & Logistics
What areas do you deliver to?
We deliver across all seven UAE emirates — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain. We also handle freight to other GCC countries for bulk orders.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery within Dubai is 1–3 business days. Deliveries to other emirates typically take 2–5 business days. We maintain a 99.2% on-time delivery record across all projects.
Can you arrange urgent or same-day delivery?
Yes, we offer priority and same-day delivery services for urgent project needs within Dubai, subject to stock availability and logistics capacity. Additional charges may apply for expedited deliveries.
Maintenance Services
What is included in your Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?
Our AMC covers scheduled preventive maintenance visits, priority response to reactive maintenance requests, detailed reporting and documentation, minor repairs within agreed scope, and 24/7 emergency support. Packages are customizable to your property type and specific needs.
How quickly do you respond to emergency maintenance?
Our emergency response team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For critical emergencies (flooding, power failure, structural damage), we guarantee a response within 2 hours in Dubai. Call us on 056 176 8617 for emergencies.
Are your maintenance technicians certified?
Yes, all our technicians are certified and licensed by relevant UAE authorities. Our electricians hold DEWA approvals, plumbers are Dubai Municipality licensed, and our HVAC engineers are certified by leading equipment manufacturers.
Pricing & Payments
How do I get a quote?
You can request a quote through our Contact page, by calling 056 176 8617, or by emailing sales@asasalenjaz.ae. For maintenance services, we typically conduct a site visit to assess the scope before providing a detailed quotation. Most quotes are provided within 24–48 hours.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, cheques, and online payments. For regular clients, we offer credit terms subject to credit assessment. All transactions are fully documented with official invoices compliant with UAE VAT regulations.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly team is always ready to help. Reach out to us directly for personalized assistance.
